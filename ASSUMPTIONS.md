# Model assumptions

- The workload, facilities, controls, forecasts and savings are simulated. Cached open data is context only; it is not evidence of measured savings.
- A deadline is the latest start hour. A step occupies one hourly capacity slot. Run-now means the earliest capacity-feasible assignment.
- Capacity is six starts per site-hour. Restricted steps remain at their origin. Critical work is common to every policy and excluded.
- A normalised accelerator group has 0.35 kW idle power, 2.8 kW maximum power and exponent 1.4. Consolidation uses 80% utilisation and a 0.78 effective-energy factor for avoided idle groups; unused groups power down. A power cap reduces speed with exponent 0.7.
- Evaporative cooling uses the supplied water equation and base PUE. Dry/chiller cooling adds 0.13 to PUE and uses 0.08 L per IT kWh. These are scenario assumptions.
- Sites B and C use carbon multipliers 0.72 and 1.18, temperature offsets -4 and +3 C, water-stress multipliers 1.8 and 2.5, and network costs of 0.08/0.16 kWh and 0.15/0.30 h.
- Off-site water is 4.52 L per facility kWh, an explicit scenario assumption.
- Forecast error is a fixed-seed bounded multiplicative perturbation. Fallback activates above 20% uncertainty. The shield rejects deadline, latency, power and restricted-routing violations.
- Confidence intervals are empirical normal intervals over ten fixed seeds. They describe simulation variation, not population uncertainty.
- The compact learning benchmark uses fixed architectures and seeds with no search. Stored results are regenerated by `train_models.py`.
- The public workload download uses timestamps and token counts only. If no stable public file is reachable, the simulator records the failure and uses its fixed synthetic workload.
- The novelty statement is a gap hypothesis, not a priority claim.
